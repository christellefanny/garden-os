# Plant Vault artwork

The approved realistic botanical style uses six locally hosted WebP atlases, each containing sixteen square images. These are AI-generated representative illustrations, not cultivar-identification photographs. Cards disclose that varieties may differ. Personal photograph URLs take precedence; failed personal photos fall back to the botanical art. Unknown plants receive a neutral leaf placeholder.

Atlas 6 adds Wildflower mix, Painted Daisy, African Daisy, Salvia, Hollyhocks, Shade flower mix, Tulips and Gladiator allium in its first two rows. The mixes illustrate representative groupings; actual seed packets can contain different species. Existing saved records receive art through name matching without reseeding or changing notes. Singular/plural aliases and Allium with the variety Gladiator are supported. Earlier tile positions, including the hot-pepper tile, remain unchanged.

Atlas 6 was created with the built-in image-generation tool using this prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: a botanical photo-style atlas for Garden OS Plant Vault, exactly FOUR equal columns by FOUR equal rows, square overall, sixteen full-bleed square tiles, no gaps borders text labels or watermarks. Tasteful professional realistic garden close-ups with natural light, detailed healthy flowers and foliage, soft green garden bokeh; not cartoons. Each subject centered and visible in central two-thirds for card cropping. Exact row-major subjects: row1: 1 wildflower seed mix (cosmos, cornflowers and daisies in a natural meadow grouping), 2 Painted Daisy (Tanacetum coccineum, pink/red ray petals and yellow discs with finely divided foliage), 3 African Daisy (Osteospermum, purple and white daisy petals and dark centers), 4 perennial Salvia with purple-blue flower spikes. Row2: 5 Hollyhocks with pink open cup flowers ascending a tall stem, 6 shade flower seed mix representative woodland grouping of forget-me-nots and columbines in dappled shade, 7 pink and pale-yellow Tulips with smooth cup-shaped blooms and strap leaves, 8 Gladiator ornamental allium with large round dense purple flower globes on tall bare stalks and basal strap leaves. Rows3 and4 repeat these SAME eight subjects in the SAME order, with slightly different natural framing. No unrelated plants, humans, pots, clutter or decorative graphics. This is representative artwork not a labeled scientific diagram.

`lib/plant-art.ts` defines row-major tile order and name aliases. All 79 distinct starter plant names have artwork, with an additional orange hot-pepper tile for Habanero and Scotch Bonnet. Artwork is lazy-loaded through Next Image. Existing records, storage keys and garden APIs are preserved.

Generation direction: realistic botanical photography appearance, natural garden light, correct leaves, fruits and flowers, healthy close-up specimens, quiet natural bokeh, consistent framing; exactly four columns and four rows with no gaps, borders, labels, text or people. Each atlas follows the ordered subjects in the mapping. Images are representative and must not be used to diagnose a plant or verify a cultivar.
