# Illustrated Flower Garden guides

Seasonal planting, Flower care and Seed saving retain all existing guidance and use distinct editorial layouts with restrained sage, blush and warm ochre accents. Seasonal planning pairs four seasons with the corresponding flower portraits and highlights the season selected in the existing theme control. Flower care includes light filters based on existing guide metadata. Seed saving includes illustrated mature seed heads and the existing collection and storage instructions. No account records or synchronization behavior changes.

## Original artwork

Created using the built-in image-generation tool with the owner's approved botanical seed illustrations as a style reference. Shared prompt: professional seed-catalog watercolor and fine colored-pencil botanical illustration; realistic plant structures and natural textures; warm ivory backgrounds; elegant, uncluttered editorial composition; no photographs, cartoons, labels, text, prices, quantities or branding.

Project-bound WebP exports, retaining the original compositions:

- `public/flower-guides/seasonal.webp`: flower seedlings, pink tulips, purple allium, bulbs, a terracotta pot, trowel and blank notebook.
- `public/flower-guides/care.webp`: a sage watering can, healthy cosmos and calendula, secateurs and a faded stem beside soil.
- `public/flower-guides/saving.webp`: dry flower heads, seeds, plain paper envelopes, a small glass jar and pencil.
- `public/flower-guides/seed-specimens.webp`: a three-column/two-row atlas, in order: zinnia, cosmos, marigold, calendula, nasturtium and dianthus mature seed structures. The prompt specifies triangular zinnia seeds; slender pointed cosmos seeds; dark/pale marigold seeds; tan curved calendula seeds; wrinkled nasturtium seeds; and dry dianthus capsules with small dark seeds. Illustrations are representative guides, not diagnostic photographs or scale diagrams.

The original generated PNGs remain in the creation workspace. Existing flower portraits are reused for seasonal and care cards. CSS blending integrates the introductory paintings with their softly colored page panels.

## Verification

Browser verification covers all 22 guidance cards, the four season selections, seed illustrations, light filters, navigation, preservation of collection storage, image decoding, WCAG AA checks and responsive widths 1440/768/390/320. Desktop and mobile screenshots are inspected before publishing.
