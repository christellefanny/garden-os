# Why Garden OS Exists

Every season, gardeners make hundreds of decisions.

- What should I plant?
- Where should I plant it?
- How many plants fit?
- When should I fertilize?
- Is this disease?
- When should I harvest?
- Which variety performed best?

Most of those decisions are forgotten by next season.

Garden OS exists to remember them all.

---

# Mission

Help gardeners spend less time remembering and more time growing.

Garden OS remembers everything so gardeners don't have to.

---

# Vision

Garden OS is the operating system for every garden.

It helps gardeners design, manage, maintain, and improve their gardens through intelligent planning, historical knowledge, and AI-powered guidance.

Garden OS should become the place gardeners open before they walk outside.

---

# Our Philosophy

Technology should disappear.

Gardeners should think about plants—not software.

Every feature should answer one question:
