# Guest kitchen garden example

Guests without a saved layout see a read-only, clearly labeled example garden on Home. Four numbered beds and matching keyboard-accessible buttons reveal design notes. The action opens the existing Plan & Design feature; visitors can then choose Create a layout. No gardens, plants, growing spaces, or layouts are created merely by browsing the example. Existing guest plans and signed-in dashboards retain their saved previews.

Artwork: `public/conservatory/kitchen-garden-example.webp` (1200 × 800, optimized WebP). Created with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized and compressed for the app. Prompt: professional botanical watercolor/gouache overhead kitchen garden on warm ivory paper; exactly four timber raised vegetable beds in a two-by-two grid, pale gravel cross paths, tomato/leafy green/root crop/pepper and bean beds, herb border, restrained raspberry and cream flowers, terracotta pots, realistic foliage and soft shadows; no text, numbers, people, greenhouse, photographs, cartoon or vector look. Labels and interactions are HTML rather than embedded in the art.

This is illustrated design inspiration, not a measured plan or local planting recommendation. The What to Plant Now section continues to provide location-specific timing separately.

Verification: guest browser flow covers the illustration decoding, all four bed selectors, WCAG A/AA checks, five viewport widths, desktop/mobile screenshots, no automatic layout persistence, and navigation through Plan & Design to the blank-layout dialog. Member navigation and the rest of the guest planting flow remain covered by the same browser run.
