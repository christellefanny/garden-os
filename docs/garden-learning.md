# Garden learning destinations

Flower Garden is nested beneath My Gardens in navigation and linked from the My Gardens overview. Its existing `?view=flower-garden` route, saved records, photos and all content remain intact. The only changes inside FlowersPage are the visible encyclopedia heading and its accessible label, now Flower Collection.

The Flower Library (`?view=flower-library`) has ten sourced plant profiles, botanical names, life cycles, light and mature-size guidance, planting, care, troubleshooting, uses and relevant cautions. Search and sunlight filters work without an account. It reuses approved botanical artwork.

Beginner’s Guide (`?view=beginners-guide`) has six guides: space, timing, soil, essentials, planting and care. The optional three-question helper offers a reading path; it explicitly does not calculate frost dates from the entered location. Checkboxes last only while the page remains open and do not claim account persistence. Guide selection moves focus to the article heading for keyboard and mobile readers.

Sources were accessed November 1, 2026: NC State Extension Plant Toolbox (one species/genus source per flower), Maryland, Minnesota, Wisconsin and Iowa State extension services, Illinois Extension annual-flower guidance, and the USDA hardiness-map explainer. Each entry links to its own sources. Copy is an original editorial summary, not a claim of professional horticultural review. Advice is scoped to U.S. outdoor home gardens and distinguishes hardiness from frost and planting conditions. Plant spacing and size are explicitly cultivar dependent rather than invented universal measurements.

No gardens or plants are created, removed or migrated. Existing guest restrictions on Plant Vault remain unchanged. The new learning pages are available to guests and accounts. Production is not updated; hosted/browser verification remains pending repository push authorization.
