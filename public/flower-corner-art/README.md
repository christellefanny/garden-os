# Garden Atelier botanical portraits

Fifteen individual botanical watercolor illustrations AI-generated on 2026-11-03. Every PNG is the unchanged native output, copied byte-for-byte into this directory. No crop, resize, paint-over, recompression or upscaling was applied.

## Resolution and integration

- All fifteen images are **1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs**, approximately 2 MB each, 33 MB together.
- Each new image is an entire individual specimen, versus roughly 313 pixels per specimen in the earlier four-column sheets.
- Use the typed mapping in `lib/flower-corner-art.ts`, keyed by profile ID. Keep the whole square in view; do not use the legacy sprite offsets or inset clipping on these portraits.
- Responsive delivery may serve appropriately sized derivatives while these originals remain unchanged.
- Exact generation prompts and depicted taxa are recorded in `prompts.json`.

## Source context

Taxa follow the corresponding Garden Atelier library profiles and their cited horticultural sources. Morphology cross-checks include [NC State Plant Toolbox](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/), [NC State on lungworts](https://extensiongardener.ces.ncsu.edu/gardening-publications-2/extgardener-previous-newsletters/extgardener-past-features/extgardener-pulmonarias-natural-choice-for-shade-gardens/), [Clemson on hellebores](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/hellebore/), and [Cambridge University Botanic Garden on Bergenia](https://www.botanic.cam.ac.uk/the-garden/horticultural-collections/national-plant-collections/bergenia/). Existing watercolor sheets were visually inspected to match the collection's style; they were not cropped to make these assets.

Care-technique visuals are intentionally separate code-native SVG schematics so cut locations and conditional species guidance can remain explicit and editable. No raster care diagram is included here.
